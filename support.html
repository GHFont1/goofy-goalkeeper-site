Goofy Goalkeeper

Big glove. Bigger glory.

How to play

Drag left or right to move the glove. Save soccer balls, dodge boots, and catch bonus stars. Every five consecutive saves increases your multiplier, up to 4×. A boot costs three points. A missed ball or caught boot resets your streak.

Matches last 60 seconds. Warm-up mode has no clock. Pause any time. The app automatically pauses when you leave it.
