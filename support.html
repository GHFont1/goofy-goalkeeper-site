How to play

Tap where the ball is going and Gus dives there. You get one dive per shot. Catch it, parry it or tip it round the post. Power shots can only be parried, curlers bend late, and clever penalty takers chip it down the middle if you dive too early.

Modes: Career (16 matches in four competitions, with a boss penalty taker at the end of each), Penalty Shootout (hold, drag to aim, release when the power marker is in the green), Daily Challenge (the same 12 shots all day), Survival (three lives) and Training (seven lessons). Level up and win trophies to unlock gloves and kits.

Settings include sound, crowd ambience, haptics, reduced motion, an assist mode with slower shots, replaying training, and resetting all progress. The game pauses automatically when you leave it.
