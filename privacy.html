Goofy Goalkeeper
Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Goofy Goalkeeper is a free offline soccer arcade game provided by Benjamin Scalzo / Great Call Athletics.

Data in the app

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or social features. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Local storage

Your best score and sound setting are stored on your device. You can reset your best score in the in-game help screen or remove all local app data by uninstalling the app. Device or operating-system backup features may follow your own system settings.

Support and website

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard technical request information under its own privacy statement. The installed game works without this website.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
