YOU’VE GOT THIS.

Drag anywhere on the pitch to move your glove. On a keyboard, use ← and →. Catch soccer balls as they reach the glove line.

Each save earns 1 point. Every 5 consecutive saves raises your multiplier, up to 4×. Catch a star for 5× your multiplier. Boots steal 3 points and reset your streak. Missed balls reset your streak.

A match lasts 60 seconds. Warm-up has no timer or saved record. Pause anytime; leaving the app automatically pauses play.
