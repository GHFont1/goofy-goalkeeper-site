How to play

Diving

Tap anywhere on the goal and your keeper dives there. You get one dive per shot, so pick your moment.

Reading the striker

Strikers lean toward their side late in the run-up. Early clubs are honest. Later ones lie. Bosses like Señor Panenka chip it down the middle if you dive before the kick.

Shot types

Placed shots find the corners. Power shots are too hot to hold, so parry them. Curlers bend late. Knuckleballs wobble. Chips float over early divers. Low drives skim the grass.

Saves and scoring

Catch it for 100, parry it for 80, fingertip it for 150. A late reflex save adds 50, a penalty save adds 50. Consecutive saves raise your multiplier up to 3x.

Modes

Career: 16 matches across four competitions, three stars each. Shootout: take and save penalties. Daily: the same 12 shots all day. Survival: three lives, endless shots. Training: seven lessons with Coach Pickles.

Gear

Level up and win trophies to unlock gloves with better reach, grip or spring, plus new kits.
